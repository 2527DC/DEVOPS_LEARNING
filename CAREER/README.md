# CAREER

Everything about the job hunt, separate from the study material.

## Where to put things

```
CAREER/
  resume/              <- PUT YOUR RESUME AND CV HERE
    resume.pdf
    cv.pdf
    portfolio.md       <- links: GitHub, Vercel deployments, live projects
  ROADMAP-6-MONTH.md   <- the plan
  applications.md      <- companies applied to, stage, outcome
  positioning.md       <- how you describe yourself, and why
```

**Drop your resume, CV and portfolio links into `CAREER/resume/`.** Any format —
PDF, docx, markdown, or just a link in `portfolio.md`. Once it is there,
`/learning-check CAREER/` will audit it: whether what you claim matches what the
banks show you can actually defend, which is exactly what a real interviewer
checks.

## On the ₹1 Cr target — read this once

₹1 Cr total compensation in India is real, but it sits at **8+ years**, or at
5–6 years for exceptional engineers at the highest-paying firms. It is not
reachable from 2 years of experience in 6 months, and any plan that promises
otherwise is selling something.

What **is** reachable in 6 months at 7 hrs/day: a **strong mid-level
DevOps/Platform role at a good product company, ₹18–35 LPA.**

That is not a downgrade of the goal — it is the only route to it. The ₹1 Cr
roles hire people who have *operated* production systems for years, and you
cannot get that experience from a laptop. You get it from the seat that the
6-month plan wins you. Take that seat, do 3–4 years of real ownership, and the
1 Cr conversation becomes normal.

**So the plan optimises for two things at once:**
1. Getting the ₹18–35 LPA offer in 6 months (the immediate goal)
2. Building the foundations the 1 Cr interviews eventually test — depth,
   failure modes, blast-radius thinking — rather than the shortcuts that pass a
   screen and stall a career

## The commands

| Command | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `/interview <topic>` | Get tested. Scored, logged, no repeated questions. |
| `/practice <topic>` | Get a hands-on lab. Build, break, debug. |
| `/learning-check <folder>` | Audit whether the studying is actually working. |
| `/ai-update <scope>` | Stay current on AI tooling without chasing hype. |

Their output accumulates in `INTERVIEW/bank/`, `PRACTICE/`, `LEARNING-CHECK/`
and `AI-TOOLING/`. Those four directories are the evidence trail — six months
from now they are also your interview stories.
