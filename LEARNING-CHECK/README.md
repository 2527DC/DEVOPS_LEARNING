# LEARNING-CHECK

Audits of your own study material. Run `/learning-check <folder>`.

## What it does

Reads a folder of your notes, then cross-references it against:

- `INTERVIEW/bank/` — what you can actually defend under pressure
- `PRACTICE/` — what you have actually built and broken
- `CAREER/resume/` — what you claim publicly

**The disagreement between those is the finding.** Notes say what you read. The
interview bank says what you can defend. The practice bank says what you can
build. When they disagree, the lowest one is the truth.

## What it reports

1. Headline — one honest sentence
2. Five scores 0–10: Coverage, Depth, **Accuracy**, Practicality, Currency
3. **Accuracy findings first** — anything written down that is wrong, quoted and
   corrected. A wrong note is worse than a missing one, because it gets
   confidently repeated in an interview.
4. Theory-vs-practice table per topic
5. The questions an interviewer would ask *because of how your notes are written*
6. Whether the system is working: score trends, unticked drills, repeat problems
7. Ranked improvement plan
8. What to **stop** doing

## Cadence

Run it monthly per active topic, and at every roadmap gate. Repeat audits on the
same folder open by comparing against the previous report — what improved, what
did not, and what was recommended and ignored. That comparison is the most
honest feedback in the system.
